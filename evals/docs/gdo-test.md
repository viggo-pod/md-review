# Game Overview: 《Tidebound》

## Executive Summary

Tidebound is a nautical survival crafting game for PC and mobile. Players sail, fish, craft, and fight sea monsters. The game combines survival mechanics with a serene ocean aesthetic. Development is planned for 18 months with a team of 25. The game targets players who enjoy open-world survival games. We believe Tidebound will stand out through its dynamic weather system.

## Core Concept

Players control a ship captain rebuilding a fleet after a storm destroys their home port. Core loop: sail → gather → craft → upgrade ship → explore new regions.
