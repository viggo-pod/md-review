# Business Requirements: Warehouse Management System

## Business Goals

The warehouse management system must reduce picking errors and speed up order fulfillment. We expect the new system to improve operational efficiency.

## Problem Statement

Current warehouse operations rely on paper lists and manual data entry. Picking errors cost the company money and delay shipments.

## Business Value

- Picking error rate reduction is expected to save significant costs per year
- Faster fulfillment should increase customer retention

## Market Analysis

The warehouse software market is worth $10 billion. Our company ships 50,000 orders per day, growing 30% year over year.

## Scope

The project covers inbound, outbound, and inventory management. (Scope boundaries not further defined.)

## Constraints

- Budget: $1.5M
- Timeline: 12 months

## Assumptions

- The operations team will adopt the new system
- Integration with the existing ERP is feasible

## Requirements

- BR-1: Scan-based picking (Must)
- BR-2: Real-time inventory visibility (Must)
- BR-3: Voice-guided packing (Could)

## Success Metrics

- Reduce picking error rate
- Reduce order fulfillment time

## Risk Analysis

Risk: operations staff resist change. Mitigation: training program.

## Dependencies

- ERP integration
- Barcode hardware procurement
